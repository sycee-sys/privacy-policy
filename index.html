Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Sycee ("Sycee", "we", "us", or "our") is a personal finance management application that helps you track transactions, manage accounts, plan budgets, monitor savings goals, and understand your financial activity.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Sycee collects, how we use it, when it may be shared with third-party service providers, and the choices available to you.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

When you create or use a Sycee account, we may collect information such as:

  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • User account identifier
  • Authentication information associated with your account

Sycee uses Firebase Authentication to provide account registration and authentication. Authentication credentials such as passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication and are not stored by Sycee's application servers.

1.2 Financial Information

When you use Sycee, you may provide financial information including:

  • Account names and account types
  • Account balances
  • Transactions
  • Transaction amounts and dates
  • Categories and tags
  • Budgets
  • Saving goals
  • Books and related financial records
  • Currency and other financial preferences

This information is used to provide Sycee's core personal finance features.

1.3 Device and Technical Information

Sycee and the third-party services it uses may process limited technical information necessary to operate the application, such as:

  • Device and operating system information
  • Application version
  • Network information such as IP address
  • Authentication and session information
  • Push notification registration tokens
  • Information required to maintain application security and reliability

1.4 Advertising Information

Sycee uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Depending on your device settings, location, applicable laws, and consent choices, AdMob may process information such as:

  • Advertising identifiers
  • Device information
  • IP address
  • Approximate location derived from network information
  • Information about advertisements displayed or interacted with
  • App and advertising-related interactions

Sycee does not provide your financial records, such as transaction details, account balances, budgets, or saving goals, to AdMob for advertising personalization.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Create and manage your Sycee account.
  • Authenticate your identity and maintain your sessions.
  • Provide financial management features.
  • Store and synchronize your financial data when synchronization is enabled.
  • Restore financial records when you request a restore.
  • Maintain your subscription entitlement.
  • Send service-related notifications.
  • Display and measure advertisements.
  • Maintain application security and prevent abuse.
  • Diagnose technical problems and improve application reliability.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal financial information.

3. Local Storage and Synchronization

Financial data may be stored on your device. When you sign in and use synchronization features, relevant data may be transmitted to our backend for synchronization and restoration.

When you sign in and use synchronization features, relevant data may be transmitted to Sycee's backend servers and stored for the purpose of providing synchronization and data restoration.

Data transmitted between Sycee and its backend services is protected using encryption in transit.

Your financial data is associated with your Sycee account so that it can be synchronized and restored for that account.

4. Financial Information

Financial information is used to provide features such as:

  • Transaction tracking
  • Account and asset management
  • Budget planning
  • Saving goals
  • Financial analytics and insights
  • Transaction restoration and synchronization

Sycee does not use your financial records to determine your creditworthiness or make lending decisions.

Sycee does not provide your financial records to advertisers for advertising personalization.

5. Firebase Services

Sycee uses Firebase services provided by Google.

Firebase Authentication

Firebase Authentication is used for account registration, sign-in, and authentication.

Firebase may process information necessary to provide authentication services, such as your email address, user identifier, authentication information, and related technical information.

Firebase Cloud Messaging

Sycee may use Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) to deliver push notifications.

FCM may process information such as your device's push notification registration token and technical information required to deliver notifications.

Push notifications may be used for service-related events, such as synchronization and transaction restoration.

6. Advertising

Sycee uses Google AdMob to provide advertisements.

AdMob may process device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location, app information, and advertising interactions to provide and measure advertising services.

Where required by applicable law, Sycee uses Google's consent mechanisms to obtain and manage your choices regarding personalized advertising.

You may be able to change your advertising privacy choices through the advertising privacy controls provided by Sycee or Google, depending on your location and applicable requirements.

Sycee does not send transaction descriptions, account balances, budgets, saving goals, or other financial records to AdMob for the purpose of selecting advertisements.

7. Subscriptions and Payments

Sycee may offer paid features or subscriptions through Google Play Billing.

Payments are processed by Google Play. Sycee does not receive or store your full payment card number or other complete payment credentials.

Sycee may receive information necessary to verify and maintain your subscription entitlement, such as purchase or subscription identifiers and subscription status.

This information is used to provide the features associated with your subscription and maintain the correct subscription status for your Sycee account.

8. Third-Party Service Providers

Sycee uses third-party services to provide specific functionality.

  • Firebase Authentication — account registration and authentication.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging — push notification delivery.
  • Google AdMob — advertising and advertising measurement.
  • Google Play Billing — subscription purchases and payment processing.
  • Google Play Services — Android platform and application functionality.
  • Cloud infrastructure providers — hosting and operating Sycee's backend services.

These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and the services they provide.

Sycee does not authorize third-party service providers to use your financial records for purposes unrelated to providing the services described in this Privacy Policy.

9. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

These measures may include:

  • Encryption in transit
  • Secure authentication mechanisms
  • Access controls
  • Secure storage of authentication credentials
  • Separation of user data
  • Other security measures appropriate to the nature of the information

No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as necessary to provide Sycee's services, maintain your account, provide synchronization and restoration, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

Financial data stored locally on your device may remain there until you delete the data, clear the application's data, uninstall the application, or otherwise remove it.

Server-side account data is retained while your account remains active, unless deletion is requested or a different retention period is required by applicable law.

11. Account and Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your Sycee account and associated data.

When an account is deleted, we will delete the personal and financial data associated with that account, subject to information that we are legally required to retain.

Some information may be retained for a limited period when necessary for legal compliance, security, fraud prevention, accounting, or other legitimate legal purposes.

Deleting the Sycee application from your device does not necessarily delete server-side account data.

If account deletion is available within Sycee, you may use the account deletion functionality provided there. You may also contact us using the contact information below to request account and data deletion.

12. Your Choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have choices or rights regarding your personal information, including:

  • Accessing information associated with your account.
  • Requesting correction of inaccurate information.
  • Requesting deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Managing notification permissions through your device settings.
  • Managing advertising privacy and personalization choices.
  • Choosing whether to use account synchronization.
  • Withdrawing consent where processing is based on consent.

Disabling certain permissions or deleting certain information may affect the availability of related Sycee features.

13. Children's Privacy

Sycee is not intended for children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

14. International Data Transfers

Sycee and its service providers may process or store information in countries other than your country of residence.

Where required by applicable law, appropriate safeguards will be used for international transfers of personal information.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to Sycee, our services, applicable laws, or our data practices.

When material changes are made, we may provide appropriate notice through Sycee, our website, or other reasonable means.

The "Last updated" date at the beginning of this Privacy Policy indicates when it was most recently revised.
