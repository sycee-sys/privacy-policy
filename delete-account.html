Delete Sycee Account

This page is for users of Sycee – Expense & Budget Tracker, a personal finance management application.

Sycee is developed and published by Sycee Financial Solution.

If you wish to delete your Sycee account and associated data, you can do so in the following ways:

Option 1: In-App Deletion

Settings → Delete Account

Option 2: Email Request

You can request account deletion by sending an email to:

service@sycee.app

Please include:

  • Your account email
  • Request for Sycee account deletion

What Happens Next

  • Your Sycee account will be deleted
  • Your associated personal data will be removed, subject to any legally required data retention obligations
  • This process may take a few days

Contact

For questions about account deletion, please contact: service@sycee.app
